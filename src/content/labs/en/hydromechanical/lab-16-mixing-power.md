# Laboratory Work №16. Determining the Power Consumption of Mechanical Mixing in a Liquid Medium

## Theoretical Background

Mechanical mixing in a liquid medium is used for forming emulsions and suspensions, and for intensifying heat and mass transfer processes. The efficiency of mixing is determined by the power consumption for rotating the agitator, which is necessary to achieve the required technological result.

## Procedure

Before starting the work, we determine the diameter of the agitator, the diameter of the vessel, and the physical characteristics of the medium under study: temperature, density, and dynamic viscosity coefficient.

We turn on the electric motor and set the specified rotational speed of the agitator's shaft using the autotransformer's handle. From the scale's dial we determine the circumferential forces corresponding to the agitator's torques at the given rotational speeds. In the table we record the values of the rotational speed n and the circumferential force P. We turn off the electric motor. The other quantities are calculated using the corresponding formulas given in the table.
