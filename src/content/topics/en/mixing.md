## Topic Overview

Mixing is used across food manufacturing: dissolving sugar and salt, preparing syrups, sauces, mayonnaise, dairy mixes, cocoa suspensions, tomato products, creams and fillings. This topic connects product properties, impeller type, flow regime and drive power with the real technological purpose of the operation.
