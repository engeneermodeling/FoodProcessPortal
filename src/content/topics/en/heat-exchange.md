## Topic Overview

Heat transfer controls food safety, product quality and energy efficiency. This block covers heat balances, overall heat-transfer coefficient, temperature driving force, heat-exchanger selection, pasteurisation, cooling, evaporation and condensation using examples from milk, juices, beer, sauces, purees and process water.
