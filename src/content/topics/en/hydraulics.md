## Overview

Hydraulics form the basis for understanding fluid flow in food production facilities. From dairy plants to breweries — liquids transport through pipelines driven by pumps. This topic covers hydrostatic and hydrodynamic laws, pipeline calculations, and pump selection and sizing.
