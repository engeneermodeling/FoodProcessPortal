# Lecture 14. Condensation, Cooling and Refrigeration Processes

## Learning Outcomes

After this lecture you will be able to explain condensation in steam and refrigeration systems, build a cooling heat balance, describe the vapour-compression cycle, select a cooling medium and connect cooling rate with food safety and quality.

---

## 1. Condensation

Condensation releases latent heat as vapour becomes liquid. It appears when steam heats a heat exchanger, vapour from an evaporator is condensed, refrigerant condenses in a refrigeration plant, or water vapour condenses on cold surfaces.

---

## 2. Cooling Balance

```text
Q = G · c · (t_in - t_out)
```

Cooling slows microorganisms, chemical reactions and enzymatic activity, stabilises fat, proteins, crystals and texture, and defines storage conditions.

---

## 3. Quality Window

![Quality window during cooling](/images/lectures/heat-exchange/cooling-quality-window.svg)

After heat treatment, the product should pass the microbial risk temperature zone quickly. Slow cooling may undermine otherwise correct pasteurisation or cooking.

---

## 4. Cooling Media

![Cooling media in food processing](/images/lectures/heat-exchange/cooling-media-map.svg)

| Medium | Use | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Water** | pre-cooling, condensers | available but temperature-limited |
| **Ice water** | milk, beverages, pasteurisation systems | about `0...2 °C` |
| **Glycol / brine** | below `0 °C` | toxicity, corrosion and leakage control |
| **Air** | cold rooms, tunnels, freezing | low heat-transfer coefficient |
| **Refrigerant** | refrigeration evaporator | efficient but safety-critical |

---

## 5. Refrigeration Cycle

![Vapour-compression refrigeration cycle](/images/lectures/heat-exchange/refrigeration-cycle.svg)

The evaporator absorbs heat, the compressor raises pressure and temperature, the condenser rejects heat, and the expansion valve lowers pressure.

Further reading: [Tetra Pak Dairy Processing Handbook: Service systems](https://dairyprocessinghandbook.tetrapak.com/chapter/service-systems) and [GEA refrigeration and heating for food processes](https://www.gea.com/en/heating-refrigeration/food/).

---

## Example

Cool `1500 kg/h` of beverage from `75 °C` to `6 °C`, `c = 3900 J/(kg·K)`.

```text
G = 1500 / 3600 = 0.417 kg/s
Q = 0.417 · 3900 · (75 - 6) ≈ 112 kW
```

Heat regeneration can reduce the refrigeration load.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Condensation releases latent heat and is central to steam and refrigeration systems.
- Cooling affects safety, shelf life and texture.
- The refrigeration cycle moves heat from low to higher temperature using compressor work.
- Cooling media are selected by temperature, safety, cost and contact risk.
- Efficient cooling starts with regeneration, insulation and good control.
