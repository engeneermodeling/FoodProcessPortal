# Lecture 26. Conveying, Dosing and Storage of Bulk Food Materials

## Learning Outcomes

After this lecture you will be able to explain why powders do not behave like dry liquids, distinguish mass flow and funnel flow, identify segregation and arching risks, select basic conveying options, and compare volumetric and gravimetric feeding.

---

## 1. Bulk Solids Are Complicated

Flour, sugar, salt, milk powder, cocoa, spices, cereals, granules and agglomerated powders can flow, arch, segregate, dust, cake, charge electrostatically, absorb moisture or break during transport.

Poor handling leads to recipe errors, lumps, dust, unstable packing, poor dissolution and contamination risk.

---

## 2. Hopper Flow

![Bulk flow patterns](/images/lectures/mechanical/bulk-flow-patterns.svg)

Mass flow means most of the stored material moves. Funnel flow means only a central channel moves while stagnant zones remain near the walls. Arching or ratholing interrupts discharge and is driven by moisture, fines, fat, sugar, electrostatics, particle shape and hopper geometry.

---

## 3. Segregation

Segregation separates particles by size, density or shape during pouring, vibration, pneumatic transport, silo filling or packing. It can make the first and last parts of a batch different, disturb microingredient distribution and destabilize dosing.

---

## 4. Conveying Options

![Conveying options](/images/lectures/mechanical/conveying-options.svg)

Belt conveyors are gentle and visible. Screw conveyors are compact and can feed but may damage products. Bucket elevators lift vertically. Vibratory feeders suit short controlled transfers. Pneumatic conveying can operate in dilute phase or dense phase; dense phase is usually gentler for fragile granules and agglomerates.

---

## 5. Feeding and Dosing

![Feeder accuracy](/images/lectures/mechanical/feeder-accuracy.svg)

Volumetric feeders deliver approximately constant volume, so mass flow changes with bulk density. Gravimetric feeders control mass. A loss-in-weight feeder measures the decreasing hopper weight and adjusts feed rate continuously.
