# Lecture 29. Dosing, Filling and Packaging of Food Products

## Learning Outcomes

After this lecture you will be able to describe a packaging line, compare filling methods, explain dose accuracy and identify key controls after sealing.

---

## 1. Packaging as a Process Step

Packaging is not just a wrapper. It creates the sale unit, protects the product, carries labelling and traceability, and verifies net weight and package integrity.

![Packaging line flow](/images/lectures/mechanical/packaging-line-flow.svg)

---

## 2. Feeding and Filling

Stable feeding is essential before filling. Powders may use hoppers and augers, liquids use pumps, viscous products use piston depositors, and piece products may use conveyors and multihead weighers.

![Filling methods](/images/lectures/mechanical/filling-methods.svg)

Volumetric filling is simple but depends on density. Gravimetric filling is better for variable products. Augers suit powders, piston fillers suit pastes, pumps suit liquids and multihead weighers suit pieces, snacks and frozen foods.

---

## 3. Sealing and Inspection

Packaging formats include bags, trays, bottles, cups, cartons, vacuum packs and modified-atmosphere packs. Seal quality depends on clean sealing surfaces, temperature, time, pressure and material behaviour.

![Package quality control](/images/lectures/mechanical/package-quality-control.svg)

After packaging, the product may pass through checkweighing, seal inspection, metal detection or X-ray inspection, labelling checks and rejection. Reject systems must be tested, not assumed.
